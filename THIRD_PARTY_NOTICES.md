# Third-party components

## Archify

`src/agent_toolkit/data/skills/archify/` contains Archify v3.0.1 from [tt-a1i/archify](https://github.com/tt-a1i/archify/tree/v3.0.1/archify), distributed under the MIT license. Its original `LICENSE` and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` are preserved in that directory. This vendored copy is used to create and validate interactive architecture diagrams; Node.js 18 or newer is required when invoking Archify.
